A web-based inquiry system for all users: check real-time drug availability at community pharmacies around Tarlac Provincial Hospital, then get directions. Staff record sales and manage inventory through POS. Pharmacies operating inside TPH are not included.

Scope

Community pharmacies around Tarlac Provincial Hospital are in scope. Pharmacies inside TPH are out of scope. The service is for all users, not a special-priority group.

Medicine safety tips

Take medications exactly as directed — on time and with or without food as specified.
Filling all prescriptions at one pharmacy helps prevent dangerous drug interactions.
Complete all antibiotic courses even if you feel better, to prevent resistance.
Avoid self-medicating. Ask a pharmacist before combining OTC drugs with prescriptions.
Keep medicines in a cool, dry place out of reach of children. Dispose of expired medicines properly.
Turn on lights when taking medicine to avoid dosage errors.
